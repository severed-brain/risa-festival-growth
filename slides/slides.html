Explicit Bets: Where We Double Down
  • Double Down #1 — Campus Ambassadors (57% of direct budget): Highest predictability and peer trust across Mumbai's 40 target colleges.
  • Double Down #2 — College Club Tie-ups + Ticket Split: Bulk cohorts lock in high show-up. In CampusFest 2025, College Partnerships achieved 85.6% show-up (₹80.52 CPV) and Referrals achieved ₹69.09 CPV vs. Paid Social at ₹273.53 CPV.
  • Dependency & Synergy: Ticket Split captures email/WhatsApp consent early so Rank 5 Drip converts hesitant leads before Dec 22.
Explicitly Deprioritized / Rejected Channels:
• Paid OOH Billboards: Too expensive for fixed student budget, zero attribution or rapid iteration loop.
• LinkedIn Ads: Wrong demographic & low intent for college cultural festivals; weak CPM efficiency vs. peer channels.
